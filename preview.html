Do's & Don'ts

✅ Do

  • Use cream #F7F4EF as the canvas — never white or cold gray.
  • Limit red to purposeful elements: hero, CTAs, scores, active states.
  • Uppercase + ≥1.4px letter-spacing on every eyebrow label.
  • Use the warm line border to separate cards — not shadows.
  • Weight 900 for hero titles; weight 800 for section titles.
  • Give Final-round content the amber treatment.

❌ Don't

  • Don't put rgba(255,255,255,x) text on cream — invisible.
  • Don't use pure black — use ink #141414 or panel.
  • Don't use more than one accent color per screen.
  • Don't set border-radius below 12px on interactive elements.
  • Don't add box-shadows to list cards — borders suffice.
  • Don't skip the 22px gap between major sections.
